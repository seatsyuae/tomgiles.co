About Tom Giles

Sport, people, and the moments that matter.

Tom Giles has spent more than 13 years in sport, in senior commercial roles at Chelsea FC, MCC at Lord’s and Bath Rugby, before cofounding Seatsy to make the best seats in sport and music effortless to book.

Tom is Founder and Managing Director of Seatsy, which he built as an equal partnership with Chairman Lewis Allsopp. Seatsy launched on 24 March 2026, is approved as an Official Ticket Seller by Dubai’s Department of Economy and Tourism (DET). The business runs from Dubai with a UK office, offering curated experiences from the Ryder Cup at Adare Manor to the Six Nations in Dublin.

Before Seatsy he founded Thrive Sports Co. Tom has continued offering premium sports travel itineraries, now under the Seatsy brand umbrella as Seatsy Travel. He also cofounded Investment Group Bath, Bath’s only angel group. Tom is a dad of three, ran nine marathons in nine days for CureSHANK in 2025, inspired by his son Jenson, and is now training for a full Ironman.

Run for Jenson · CureSHANK

Nine marathons. Nine days. One reason.

9marathons in 9 days
392 kmBath to Land’s End
£24,350raised for CureSHANK
202527 June to 5 July

Tom’s son Jenson has Phelan McDermid Syndrome, a rare genetic condition. In the summer of 2025 Tom ran from Bath to Land’s End: nine marathons on nine consecutive days, about 392 km, from 27 June to 5 July.

“Run for Jenson” raised £24,350 for CureSHANK, the charity funding research into Phelan McDermid Syndrome, and sparked CureSHANK’s global Race for Research campaign. The run was covered by BBC News, GB News, Somerset Live, The News International and The Sun, and Tom is now training for a full Ironman.
