Tom Giles on sport, hospitality and building a business between Dubai and the UK

Founder and Managing Director of Seatsy, the premium sports and music hospitality marketplace. 13+ years inside MCC at Lord’s, Chelsea FC and Bath Rugby. Dad of three, angel investor, and the runner who did nine marathons in nine days for his son Jenson.

What Tom talks about

Honest, energetic and practical. Tom records remotely or in person in Dubai and the UK, and is happy to go long form.

  1. Premium hospitality, done properly

    What people actually buy when they book a box, a lounge or a full weekend away, why trust is the whole product, and why Seatsy only sells hospitality sourced from official and certified resellers.

  2. Building a marketplace from Dubai for the world

    Launching in 2026, becoming an Official Ticket Seller approved by Dubai’s Department of Economy and Tourism, and building a UK office.

  3. Sports partnerships from the inside

    Lessons from his Bath Rugby years as Head of Business Development, with partners such as Thatchers, Dyson, Jaguar Land Rover and Opro, and what rights holders get wrong about hospitality.

  4. Founder life between the UK and Dubai

    Running one business across two time zones and two very different markets, the Gulf appetite for live sport, and building a team in Dubai and the UK.

  5. Nine marathons in nine days for Jenson

    Running from Bath to Land’s End for CureSHANK, raising awareness of Phelan McDermid Syndrome, and what an endurance challenge teaches you about pace, patience and people. Now training for a full Ironman.

  6. From employee to founder

    Leaving a job in sport, founding Thrive Sports Co, and evolving it into Seatsy Travel, where Tom has continued offering premium sports travel itineraries under the Seatsy brand umbrella.

  7. Angel investing as an operator

    Why Tom cofounded Investment Group Bath, Bath’s only angel group, and what founders can learn from sitting on the investor side of the table.

  8. Mental health in sport and business

    Tom cocreated Bath Rugby’s Tunnel Mentality podcast for Mental Health Awareness Week in 2021. A good fit for wellbeing and leadership shows.

Long bio · 150 words

Tom Giles is Founder and Managing Director of Seatsy, the premium sports and music hospitality marketplace he built with Lewis Allsopp. Seatsy launched on 24 March 2026, is based in Dubai with a UK office, and is approved by Dubai’s Department of Economy and Tourism as an Official Ticket Seller.

Tom has spent more than 13 years in sport, starting at MCC at Lord’s and moving on to senior commercial roles at Chelsea FC and Bath Rugby, where he rose from Community Engagement Manager to Head of Business Development. At Chelsea he helped corporate partners such as Yokohama Tyres, Hyundai, EA Sports and Hublot with commercial activations, especially on matchdays. At Bath Rugby he was part of partnership deals with brands including Thatchers, Dyson, Jaguar Land Rover and Opro. He then founded Thrive Sports Co and has continued offering premium sports travel itineraries, now under the Seatsy brand umbrella as Seatsy Travel, and cofounded Investment Group Bath, Bath’s only angel group.

A dad of three, Tom ran nine marathons in nine days from Bath to Land’s End in 2025 for CureSHANK and his son Jenson, who has Phelan McDermid Syndrome. He is now training for a full Ironman, and launches his own podcast, Flat Out with Tom Giles, in January 2027.
